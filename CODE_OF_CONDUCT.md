# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss ideas and implementation choices without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats and publication of another person's private information are not welcome.

Keep issues and discussions relevant to TeamSwarm. Explain reproduction steps and the outcome you need. Respect differing experience levels and language backgrounds.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when necessary to keep the project usable. For a concern involving private information, request a private contact channel without repeating that information publicly.
